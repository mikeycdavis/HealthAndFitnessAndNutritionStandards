## What

The two source prompts committed verbatim and never edited again, the architecture record, six
ADRs, the derived numbered specification, and the inventory and fidelity guards.

## Why

A derived specification that nobody can check against its source is a rewrite. The two guards are
what make "reproduced verbatim" an assertion the repository can fail on rather than a claim in
prose.

## Notes

The inventory guard was deliberately written *before* any standard existed, so that a silently
skipped standard number could never reach a commit. `d6d6496` normalised line endings to LF for
the same reason: the guards compare text, and CRLF drift would have made byte-fidelity checks
meaningless on Windows.
