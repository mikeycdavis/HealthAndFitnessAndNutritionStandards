## What

Build the standards pack, then establish by independent review and recorded human attestation
that it is fit to be adopted — and only then tag it.

## Why

Certification is what makes a version adoptable, not completeness. The implementation has been
finished since `cdda7d6`; that is not the same thing as being releasable.

## Notes

The two halves are deliberately separate epics. EP-01 is engineering and is closed. EP-02 is
evidence, and it is not something an agent can finish on its own — the remaining step requires a
real human identity on an attestation.
