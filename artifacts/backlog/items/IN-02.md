## What

Make it structurally impossible for a project to represent itself as standards-compliant without
being evaluated, or to cross a release boundary without satisfying these standards.

## Why

Adherence currently depends on someone remembering to consult the standards on every project. The
gap this closes is not coverage of the domain — it is that the pack has no way of finding a project
that never adopted it.

The end state:

> A project can contain health, fitness, or nutrition work while non-compliant. It cannot
> accidentally represent itself as standards-compliant, and it cannot cross a release boundary
> without satisfying these standards.

## Notes

**Deferred until 1.0.0 certification completes.** This initiative creates no obligation to release
and must not modify the certification candidate.

Design principle: **centrally governed, locally declared, automatically enforced.** This repository
owns normative truth. An adopter owns applicability, evidence, permitted exceptions, and
attestations — never a copy of the standards, or there will eventually be twenty subtly different
versions of "the standards".
