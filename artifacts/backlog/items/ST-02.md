## What

Review all four worked interpretation records unabridged against the tier definitions, remediate,
and re-review from a focused diff.

## Why

The highest-consequence rule in the pack. Tier language is what a reader acts on.

## Notes

The corpus could not be an extract: calibration is a property of a whole record, so all four
records went in complete and in tier order.

The finding was safety-significant. The tier-four record under-escalated — exertional chest
discomfort with disproportionate breathlessness, framed so that a reader might reasonably wait. The
underlying design gap was that Standard 3 R4 governed *tone* and said nothing about *timeframe*, so
"calm" was silently doing both jobs. Fixed in the standard, the exemplar, and the worked example:
**calm stays constant across all four tiers; urgency does not.**

No numeric threshold was introduced to make the fix look more evidence-based, and no detector was
added — tier calibration has no reliable mechanical form.
