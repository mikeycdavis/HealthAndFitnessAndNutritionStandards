## What

A human reviewer reads the four dispositions and decides, per rule, whether to record an
attestation in `project-policy.yml` under their own `reviewedBy` identity.

## Why

The dispositions are evidence offered to a decision-maker, not the decision.

## Notes

**Complete.** All four approved by Michael Davis on 2026-08-11, recorded under their own identity with
a `reviewedAgainst` digest per entry. The verdict moved to `COMPLIANT` at exit 0. Evidence:
[`attestation-2026-08-11.md`](../../release-review/attestation-2026-08-11.md), *The valid recording*.

**Closed on the second attempt.** An agent must never record an attestation or invent a `reviewedBy`
identity — doing so is the "falsify evidence for" clause of `integrity.no-standards-manipulation`,
which is the very invariant being attested around. This item was marked COMPLETE once before, at
`ad6bdcb`, on attestations recorded from a drafted decision rather than from the named person's own
judgement; they were withdrawn at `7b650f2` and the item reopened.

**The specific mistake, so it is not repeated by whoever picks this up.** A drafted decision is not a
decision. Text stating what a reviewer would conclude — however accurate, however willingly offered —
is not that reviewer concluding it. `reviewedBy` answers *who is accountable*, and the only valid
source of that answer is the accountable person. Nothing in the offering party's tone or confidence
changes that, and neither does the correctness of the content.

`project-policy.yml`'s attestations comment now names exactly the four rules the tool is waiting
on, and a test asserts that list equals `standards status --json`'s `missingEvidence`. Before
`84ff9ba` it named the integrity invariant, which is not attestable — a certifier following it
would have triggered `BLOCKED_BY_INVARIANT` at exit 3, and the system's refusal would have read as
*their* error rather than the document's.

**If any attestation is declined, stop there.** A declined attestation is new evidence about the
content. It is not a reason to loosen certification.
