## What

The catalog loader built on `kind` (`requirement | recommendation | prohibition | invariant`), the
59-rule catalog across six files, the human-reviewed rule inventory, the `rules` guard, and
`PROHIBITIONS.md`.

## Why

Prohibitions are 34 of the 59 rules in this domain, so "must never" is a type rather than a boolean
on a requirement. The rule inventory is the integrity invariant's tamper evidence: a prohibition
cannot be dropped, downgraded, or reclassified without editing a reviewed file in a visible diff.

## Notes

`PROHIBITIONS.md` is hand-written and test-checked rather than generated. A generated index is a
file people hand-edit eventually; a checked one cannot drift without failing.
