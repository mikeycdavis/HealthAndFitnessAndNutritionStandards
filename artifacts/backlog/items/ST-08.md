## What

State the principle explicitly in [Standard 2](../../../standards/02-trend-over-event.md), with
Standard 32 R5 and Standard 8 R3 pointing at it:

> **Observation cadence and decision cadence are separate choices. More frequent measurement does
> not imply more frequent interpretation or intervention.**

## Why

`standards/32-energy-balance.md` R5 says frequent weighing beats rare weighing, *provided* the
average is what is read. That is the opposite of a careless reading of the trend-over-event
principle, and the distinction it rests on is implicit in one sentence and stated nowhere else.

## Notes

**Deferred, not forgotten.** The reviewer classified it a documentation improvement, and it is
frozen until ST-05 by the evidence chain.

It would also give `standards/31-trend-based-progress.md` R3's review-cadence argument a principle
to cite rather than restating it.

**Decide the encoding before starting.** As prose inside an existing standard it does not change
the inventory. As a catalogued rule it does — and the frozen baseline is 42 standards / 59 rules /
34 prohibitions, which is not changed casually.
