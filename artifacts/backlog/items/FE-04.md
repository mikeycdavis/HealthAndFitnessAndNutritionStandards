## What

`init`, `audit`, `check`, `explain`, `status`; eight detectors bound to exactly 21 rules; the
adopter and domain templates; four worked interpretation records, one per escalation tier; and the
fixture repositories built from those examples.

## Why

`audit` gathers evidence and `check` reaches a verdict — separate jobs with separate exit-code
contracts. `init` derives its dry run and its apply from one `plan()`, so a dry run structurally
cannot diverge from the apply it predicts.

## Notes

The fixtures reuse the worked examples rather than duplicating them, which makes the examples
executable evidence instead of decoration. The `naming-only` fixture exists for one defect class:
a tier label *mentioned* in prose must not satisfy a detector looking for one *assigned*.
