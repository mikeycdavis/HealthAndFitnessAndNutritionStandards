## What

Improvements to the standards prose and worked material that were identified but deliberately not
made.

## Why

Two reasons, and both need stating: the reviewer classified each as non-blocking, *and* editing
reviewed content before the attestation is recorded would sever the evidence chain.

## Notes

The chain is *material reviewed → disposition → human attestation*. Editing the middle leaves a
human attesting material nobody reviewed in its current form. A benign normative edit invalidates
review provenance just as effectively as a self-serving one, which is why "but it's an improvement"
is not an exception here.

Nothing under this initiative may be started before ST-05 is recorded.
