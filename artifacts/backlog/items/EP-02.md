## What

Independent substantive review of the four `manual-review` rules that apply to this repository and
that no machine evaluates, followed by recorded human attestation and release mechanics.

## Why

Five guards passing and 160 tests passing establish that the machinery is sound. They establish
nothing about whether the standards prose is true. Those four rules are precisely the surface where
the repository has to be judged by a person.

## Notes

The rule the reviewer set at the outset governed everything after it: *if substantive review finds
a problem, fix the problem, not the verdict machinery.* Review found problems in three of the four
rules, and in every case the content moved rather than the rule, the tier definitions, or the
evaluator.

`7f59f8b` is the immutable certification baseline. It does not advance, including for benign
post-baseline commits — those stay in the diff and must be explained before any verdict is
inspected. The procedure is written into
[`artifacts/release-review/dispositions.md`](../../release-review/dispositions.md).
