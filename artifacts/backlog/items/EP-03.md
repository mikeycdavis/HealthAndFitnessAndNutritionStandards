## What

Eight requirements covering organisation-level discovery, self-protecting enforcement, umbrella
composition, release migration classification, one evaluation path, version pinning, bootstrap, and
test prioritisation.

## Why

It is the first post-1.0 work, ahead of adding more health rules. More rules deepen a pack nobody
is required to consult.

## Notes

**Dormant until v1.0.0 certification completes.** Implementation must not begin until the human
attestation and release process has finished successfully. `7f59f8b` remains the baseline against
which the eventual certification diff must first be explained.

The full text of all eight requirements lives in [BACKLOG.md](../../../BACKLOG.md) and is not
duplicated into the features below — those track liveness, BACKLOG.md holds the reasoning. The one
deliberate omission there is a complete cross-domain precedence ordering, which is left unfrozen on
purpose: only the dominance of `BLOCKED_BY_INVARIANT` is justified by what is currently known, and
writing the rest into a backlog entry would be making exactly the cross-domain normative decision
the requirement warns against.
