## What

Software and agents that produce health, fitness, or nutrition guidance should be held to
standards that are auditable rather than aspirational, and that can always say *we do not have
enough evidence* instead of being forced to a positive conclusion.

## Why

In this domain a false green is worse than no answer. A detector can establish that a document
contains a section called `## Measurement Quality`; it cannot establish that the analysis inside
it is sound. Everything under this theme exists to keep those two claims apart.

## Notes

The theme outlives any particular release. It stays open while the standards are maintained.
