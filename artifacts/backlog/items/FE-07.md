## What

A human decides whether the four dispositions warrant recording four attestations, and if so the
release mechanics follow: verify against the real repository, remove CI's exit-4 allowance, move
`VERSION` off `1.0.0-dev`, re-run the gate, tag `v1.0.0`.

## Why

An attestation carries a `reviewedBy` identity. Only a person can supply one.
