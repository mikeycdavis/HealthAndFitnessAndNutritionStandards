## What

`standards/02-trend-over-event.md` R2 says of the red-flag exception:

> a single observation genuinely establishes that something needs attention

Replace with wording closer to *"is sufficient to require attention"*.

## Why

Defensible as written, because the object being established is the *need for attention* rather than
a physiological conclusion. But the current wording invites reading "establishes" the way the rest
of the standard uses it, which is the sense the exception specifically does not claim — and this is
the hinge of the whole exception.

## Notes

**Deferred, not forgotten.** Non-blocking by the reviewer's classification, and frozen until ST-05
by the evidence chain.

The reviewer's formulation of why the override is coherent, worth keeping alongside the change:

> A red flag does not make one observation sufficient to establish the underlying medical pattern.
> It makes one observation sufficient to establish a **decision obligation** because the cost of
> waiting is asymmetric.
