## What

The prohibition has no worked negative example. If written, the reviewer's suggested shape:

```text
Acceptable:
"Dietary patterns are associated with cardiovascular risk."

Potentially acceptable with strong evidence:
"Regular consumption of X is associated with Y outcome."

Prohibited:
"X prevents heart disease."  /  "X cures diabetes."
```

## Why

A **documentation opportunity, not a defect** — the reviewer's classification. An adopter can
determine the prohibited boundary from Standard 14 R3, Standard 37 R1, and Standard 37 R5 without
one, and examples should demonstrate semantics rather than create them.

## Notes

**Explicitly not created by this finding: a rule that every prohibition must carry a worked
negative example.** Three others lack one — `nutrition.no-starvation-approaches`,
`nutrition.no-identical-response-assumption`, `nutrition.no-scale-change-as-fat-change`. That
invariant was considered and rejected during review. Do not reconstruct it from this item.

See ST-03 for the review that produced it.
