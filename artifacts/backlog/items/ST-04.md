## What

Build a corpus around the three places the trend-over-event principle is under tension, and review
whether the repository's statements of it stay consistent.

## Why

The principle is cited by nearly every standard, and it has a red-flag exception that could easily
read as a contradiction of the principle it sits inside.

## Notes

The corpus was organised by tension rather than by file: the cost-asymmetric single-event override,
the same measurement read at two timescales, and the cases where trend reasoning *increases*
urgency rather than damping it.

Disposition ESTABLISHABLE, with the unifying reading recorded: a red flag does not make one
observation sufficient to establish an underlying pattern — it makes one observation sufficient to
establish a **decision obligation**, because the cost of waiting is asymmetric. Two non-blocking
wording improvements came out of it and are deferred to ST-07 and ST-08.
