## What

An org-level process that enumerates repositories, identifies potential health, fitness, or
nutrition capability, detects absence of adoption, and creates an obligation for an explicit
applicability determination.

## Why

Repository-local detection is defence-in-depth *after* adoption. A repo that never adopted has no
policy, no workflow, and no required check — so there is nothing to fail.

## Notes

**Detection proposes applicability; it never establishes it.** A false-negative detector must not
be able to silently exempt a project, so the output is an obligation to answer rather than an
answer:

```text
Potential health/fitness/nutrition scope detected.
HealthAndFitnessAndNutritionStandards applicability must be explicitly established.
```
