## What

A review corpus built for each of the four rules, an independent disposition issued against it,
remediation where the disposition was DEFECTIVE, and re-review of the remediation. All four
dispositions are ESTABLISHABLE and recorded durably.

## Why

A disposition is an agent's evidence-backed review result. It is deliberately not an attestation:
recording an attestation requires a real human `reviewedBy` identity, and an agent writing one
would be the "falsify evidence for" clause of the very invariant being attested.

## Notes

Nine repository defects came out of this feature, two of them on surfaces someone acts on rather
than surfaces that merely describe — `project-policy.yml`'s attestations comment named a rule that
is not attestable, and the README stated a false definition of `COMPLIANT`. Both were found by
running the release procedure rather than by reading about it. That ordering is now recorded as
requirement 8 of EP-03.
