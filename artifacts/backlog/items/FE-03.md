## What

The policy schema and this repository's own `project-policy.yml` written in the same sitting, then
the 42 standards in batches with a guard run between each batch.

## Why

Writing a real policy is what reveals that *not-applicable* and *exception* are different claims
that must not share a mechanism. Writing the schema alone would not have surfaced it.

## Notes

Batching was a sequencing constraint rather than convenience: each batch settled vocabulary that
later documents then had to honour, and settling it after forty documents existed would have meant
rewriting forty documents.
