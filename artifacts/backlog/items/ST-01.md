## What

Extract every empirical claim the repository makes verbatim, submit the corpus for independent
review, remediate the defects found, and re-review the remediation.

## Why

The largest empirical surface in the pack, and the rule under which a fabricated medical fact would
be caught. If any rule had to be reviewed against external evidence rather than the author's own
evidence pack, it was this one.

## Notes

The review surface was initially reported as 73 claims. Manual enumeration at claim granularity,
plus the fitness standards that the first pass had not scanned at all, gave **191 entries / 182
distinct claims**. The 73 figure was discarded for certification purposes.

The reviewer refused to review from a summary — "I cannot claim a claim-level review of 73
assertions when roughly 40 are not actually before me" — which is why the corpus is a committed
artifact rather than conversation.

Five blocking findings were remediated at `79d39d1`, including a rationale field in
`rules/nutrition.json` making a stronger claim than its parent standard. That one is now pinned by
a byte-identical-sentence test with a mutation case, because it is drift a reader would never spot.
