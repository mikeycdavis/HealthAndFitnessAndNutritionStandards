## What

No tooling may treat `1.0.0-dev`, `main`, or the current certification candidate as adoptable.

## Why

Certification is what makes a version adoptable, not completeness. The implementation has been
finished since `cdda7d6` and the repository is still correctly reporting `NOT_EVALUATED`.
