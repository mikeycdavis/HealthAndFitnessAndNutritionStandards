## What

`standards/41-dietary-restrictions-and-context.md` R2 names this as the one place in the series
where a mechanical check would straightforwardly earn its place. None exists.

## Why

It needs a project's food data and stored restrictions, which these detectors do not have — they
read a repository's documents, not its runtime data.

> A check that appeared to verify allergen safety without that access would be the most dangerous
> false green this system could produce, so none is offered.

## Notes

**Blocked on a design problem, not on scheduling.** Any future attempt must solve the access
problem first. A partial implementation here is worse than nothing, and this is the single item in
the backlog where shipping something incomplete could plausibly hurt someone.

The absence is documented in the standard's own Implementation section, so a reader checking
coverage does not have to guess whether it is an oversight.
